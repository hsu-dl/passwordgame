How to Play

  • Objective: Shoot the "Weak Passwords" to score points before time runs out.
  • Watch Out: Do not shoot the garbled "Strong Passwords," or you will lose a life.
  • Lives: You have 3 lives in total.
  • Timer: The game session lasts for 1 minute.
  • Controls: Click the left mouse button to "shoot" a password.

What Are "Weak Passwords"?

They are passwords that are easy for hackers to guess. Look for these types:

  • Common & Short: like password, 123456, or qwerty.
  • Personal Details: like john1995 or lisa88.
  • Single Dictionary Words: like sunflower or dragon.
